# Docs

Everything written down about Nerve, and the order to read it in.

**If you are picking up work:** read this page, then `M3-PLAN.md` for what is
next and `LAUNCH-GAP.md` for what is blocking launch. Both carry status
markers, and both are meant to be updated by whoever does the work.

**If you are trying to understand why a character behaved the way she did,**
read [`HUMANNESS.md`](HUMANNESS.md) instead. It is the only document that
describes the whole judgement machine end to end, in the present tense.

## Keeping these true

A change that ships with stale docs is not finished. The rule of thumb for
what to touch when:

| If you changed | Update |
|---|---|
| A plan item | `M2-PLAN.md` — mark it shipped, say what actually landed and what is still owed by hand |
| Anything on the interview track — a character, the setup flow, the CV, a credit, a pack price | `INTERVIEW-PLAN.md` — mark the item shipped with what landed, and check its §0 tier before you open a file. **Rule 19 of `CLAUDE.md` governs all of it**, and it is now enforced rather than remembered: `lib/characterization/dating-arm.test.ts` pins every compiled contract, band, reciprocity decision, fast-scorer delta, timing constant, rubric and reservation on the dating arm, and fails on the commit that moves one. The interview equivalent of a judgement file is a new file beside it — `lib/warmth/interview/`, `lib/grade/interview/`, `lib/personas/interview/`, `lib/safety/interview-escalation.ts` — and the seam is three selectors reading `persona.track` (`lib/warmth/track.ts`, `lib/warmth/track-prompt.ts`, `lib/grade/track.ts`) with dating as the default branch. `INTIMACY_ANCHORS` alone is why: it would score a candidate's last job mid-way up a scale whose top is sexual. **The trap that is left is accounting**, not progression: `sessions.track` now exists and every progression read filters on it, but the ledger still stamps every combined turn with the text model, so a credit's COGS cannot be attributed by model (`LAUNCH-GAP.md` §3). And a one-time Whop payload is captured from a real purchase before the handler is written (rule 14). A pack price is authored in `lib/site/plans.ts` beside `OFFERS`, and its expiry rule — purchased credits never expire, granted ones die at the period boundary — is stated in `components/site/legal-pages.tsx` in the same commit |
| A probe domain, a design brief, the difficulty ladder, or what an interviewer asks about | `INTERVIEW-TECHNICAL-PLAN.md` — its §13 is the record and **its §14 is the UI pass**; add to them rather than rewriting the argument above. Four files hold the content: `lib/data/interview-probes.ts` (domains), `lib/data/interview-briefs.ts` (problems and the design ladder), `lib/data/interview-difficulty.ts` (the five levels) and `lib/data/interview-credits.ts` (the round table). **A field is authored in `interview-fields.ts` and OFFERED only once it has probe domains** — `selectableInterviewFields` derives the picker from them, so writing the domains is what opens the field and there is no second list. Two more files decide what an interview SCORES: `lib/grade/interview/metrics.ts` is the deterministic 60% (four of the eight dating bands score correct interview behaviour at zero — the note there is the argument) and `lib/data/interview-scorecard.ts` is how those rows read on screen. **The domain is authored and the question is generated**, which is how rule 10 survives here; a directive that names the question is a script and §05 and §11 both refuse one. A design brief is the single exception and §4.3 says why. Three separations are load-bearing and each was learned somewhere else first: the difficulty slider gets **no warmth opinion** (the mistake `lib/warmth/reciprocity.ts` made), `probeShare` moves how often a beat is **due** and is never a per-turn quota (the 40% quota in `INTERVIEW-PLAN.md` §14 gagged an interviewer on four turns in five), and accuracy **abstains by default** rather than choosing between right and wrong on an ambiguous answer. A new round `shape` is a behavioural change to that round, so re-read §4 before adding one — and `technical_accuracy` is nullable on purpose, because a behavioural round has no accuracy score and null is not zero |
| The grading rubric or the grade route | Re-run `npm run grade:calibrate`. A rubric change is a calibration change, and the golden set has to be re-scored rather than quietly drifting past the threshold |
| Anything on the blocker list | `LAUNCH-GAP.md` — the entry, its size, and the totals at the top |
| A screen from fixture to real data | `INTEGRATION-GAPS.md` |
| The schema, a policy, or a `db:*` script | `DATA.md` |
| A product rule — rep length, thresholds, what a section is for | `PRODUCT.md`, and record the drift in `LAUNCH-GAP.md` §4 if it now disagrees with the spec |
| A persona dial or contract | `PERSONA.md` |
| A persona's band table, word caps, steering heartbeat, or anything else that composes into what she is told each turn | `PERSONA-AUDIT.md` — mark the defect fixed with what landed, then **re-run `npm run rep:audition`**. A cap is not a cap until it has been auditioned: an uncountable one produced a 40-word median, and a stated one comes back as roughly 1.2x. Compare against an untouched character in the same run, never against M0's gate — that gate was measured on the realtime pipeline and this harness reads higher for everybody. The harness drives `statelessDirective()` and trims at the band ceiling, so it auditions the arm that ships; watch its `capped by the band` line, which is the gap between what she wants to say and what she is allowed to |
| Any scoring rule, any warmth mechanism, or anything about what a character is allowed to decide | `HUMANNESS.md` — it is the map of every judgement in the build and goes stale the moment one of them moves |
| Whether she may ask, volunteer, or say anything at all this turn | `lib/warmth/reciprocity.ts` — pure functions, the `rep-rules.ts` pattern. **Warmth models how much she LIKES him; this models who is doing the work**, and until it existed the two came apart: at warmth 41 she answered three consecutive one-word turns with self-disclosure, a question and more self-disclosure. The mirror cap may only ever LOWER the band, never raise it, never mirrors a question, and floors a real turn at the band's typical; the question gate rides the session's `suppressQuestion` rather than adding a clause — one system still owns the question rule. Silence is enforced by making no request, never twice running, never on the closing turn, and never on his opening line. **It must never carry a warmth floor above the band table's.** Every gate here sat on ENGAGED for a day — twenty points above the band it was gating and above every `unlocksAt` on the roster — and it won silently: a rep that peaked at 56 asked nothing in seventeen turns, added nothing to any answer, and had all four of Nadia's authored gates dropped unread. `HUMANNESS.md` §7.1 is the write-up |
